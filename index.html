About us

Rooted in Manipur. Inspired by Nature.

In the lush landscapes of Kangleipak—Manipur, nature has always been more than a backdrop to life. It is part of our heritage, our traditions, and our understanding of wellness.

For generations, the people of this land have lived closely with plants, herbs and natural resources, carrying forward knowledge of their uses from one generation to the next. Kangleipak Herbs was born from our desire to honour that relationship with nature and bring the botanical richness of Manipur to a wider world.

We believe that the finest natural products begin with where they come from.

Our journey begins with carefully selected herbs and botanicals, sourced with an emphasis on authenticity, quality and responsible practices. We work towards preserving the natural character of these ingredients while bringing them to modern consumers through thoughtful processing, stringent quality standards and responsible packaging.

Our Heritage, Our Responsibility

The name Kangleipak represents our connection to the land we call home.

Our identity is inspired by the Kanglasha, the powerful mythical dragon-like guardian associated with Manipur's cultural heritage. In our logo, the guardian embraces a living plant held gently in a human hand—a symbol of protection, nature, knowledge and responsibility.

That philosophy lies at the heart of Kangleipak Herbs.

We don't see herbs simply as commodities. We see them as part of a living ecosystem and a heritage worth protecting.

From Manipur to the World

Our vision is to create a trusted platform for the natural wealth of Manipur and the Northeast of India—connecting exceptional herbs and botanical products with customers across India and, eventually, the world.

As we grow, we aim to build relationships with farmers, growers, collectors and local communities, creating opportunities while encouraging sustainable cultivation and responsible sourcing.

Every product we develop carries a simple promise:

Know where it comes from.

Know what goes into it.

Respect what nature gives us.
